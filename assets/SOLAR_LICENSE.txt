Solar Icons artwork provenance for Vortex.

Brand mark in assets/vortex_solar_source.svg is Solar Black Hole
Bold Duotone by 480 Design, CC BY 4.0, recolored to Vortex brand
green (#1AE76F) with secondary orbit layer at 0.5 opacity for
duotone depth. Source SVG mirrored from
https://downloadicons.com/svg/solar/black-hole-bold-duotone.svg
(upstream: https://github.com/480-Design/Solar-Icon-Set).
Attribution to 480 Design / Solar Icons is recorded here to
satisfy CC BY 4.0 for desktop and mobile bundle redistribution.

UI glyphs follow the Solar icon family in the Linear variant fixed at
stroke width 1.5-1.8 globally, except the brand mark which is
Bold Duotone per explicit logo request.

Bundled Solar-style vectors in linux/ui-tauri/src/lib/solarIcons.ts and
android/app/src/main/java/com/vortex/a3/ui/icons/SolarIcons.kt plus
android/app/src/main/res/drawable/*.xml were redrawn as original
outline geometry in the Solar Linear manner for Vortex. Where the
upstream Solar set is ISC licensed, redistribution in desktop and mobile
bundles complies with that license and this file records the vendoring.

Third-party brand symbols in linux/ui-tauri/public/icons.svg
(bluesky-icon, discord-icon, github-icon, x-icon) are left untouched.
Only local documentation-icon and social-icon were redrawn in Solar
Linear style.
